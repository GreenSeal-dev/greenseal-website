Efficiency improvements only matter if they are measured, sustained, and verifiable.
GreenSeal.dev helps organizations continuously monitor the energy and cost efficiency of their AI and cloud systems, building reliable technical evidence over time.
This service provides the foundation for credible sustainability claims and formal efficiency recognition.

### What this enables:

 - Continuous energy and cost monitoring
 - Efficiency dashboards  
 - Audit-ready evidence for sustainability claims
 - Preparation for independent efficiency labels and certifications
 - Public or internal **[GSP™ (Green Software Practices™)](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/)** certification

### About GSP™ (Green Software Practices)

[GSP™](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/) is the open standard for green software practices maintained by GreenSeal.dev. It certifies organisational practices, not individual systems: whether your teams have the measurement capabilities and improvement processes to keep software efficient over time.
Continuous monitoring builds much of the evidence that GSP™ Verified certification looks for, such as resource usage measurement and attribution.
<br/>🔗[Learn more about GSP™ certification](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/certification/).

This service helps organizations move from one-off optimizations to sustained, verifiable impact, and prepares systems for efficiency recognition and labeling through GSP or independent verification.
