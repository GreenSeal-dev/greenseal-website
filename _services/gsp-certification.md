[Green Software Practices (GSP)](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/) is an open standard for building energy- and resource-efficient software and AI systems. The practices are free for anyone to use. Certification against them is operated by GreenSeal.dev.

This page is about our paid certification services. The standard, the certification statuses, the terms and the registry of certified organisations are on **[gsp.greenseal.dev](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/certification/)**. Organisations starting out can get the free [GSP™ Self-assessed](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/self-assessment/) status on their own.

### GSP™ Verified

For organisations that want external credibility, for example to back public sustainability claims.

- Independent review by GreenSeal.dev of your evidence against the [Level 2 practices](https://gsp.greenseal.dev/practices/?level=2)
- GSP™ Verified badge and public certification page, awarded only if the criteria are met
- Technical assessment report with identified gaps and recommendations, regardless of the outcome
- Optional public summary report
- Valid for 12 months

You pay for an independent verification, not for a badge. If the criteria are not met, you keep your current status and get a clear report on what to improve before requesting re-verification.

### GSP™ Verified+

For organisations aiming for leadership: a custom engagement, 6 to 12 months recommended, that ends with a formal verification.

- Technical and organisational guidance
- A tailored sustainability and efficiency roadmap
- Internal enablement: workshops, keynotes, AMA sessions and knowledge transfer
- Regular check-ins to monitor progress, with actionable reports

All participating organisations receive a **GSP™ Verified+ In Progress** badge during the engagement. The GSP™ Verified+ badge is awarded only on successful completion of the engagement and a formal verification review. Organisations that don't meet the criteria at the end don't receive the badge, but keep the benefits of the engagement.
